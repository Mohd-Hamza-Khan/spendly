Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

Spendly is designed to minimize data collection. We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service: your email address and password for account creation. Your expense data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Data

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your access to the application
  • Improve and optimize our service

Data Storage

Your expense data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms. We do not maintain any external databases containing your personal financial information. You are responsible for backing up your data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We do not share your data with third-party advertisers or marketing services. Your data remains exclusively under your control.

Cookies

Spendly does not use cookies or tracking technologies. We respect your privacy and do not monitor your browsing activity.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Your password is securely hashed before storage. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Our service may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over the content or practices of these sites and assume no responsibility for their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of Spendly constitutes acceptance of these changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@spendly.app
